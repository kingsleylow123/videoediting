What I found in the raw file

  • Captions are burned in, in English and Chinese, and they drift. They sync at the start but run about 15s behind the audio by the end. That's why I need the clean original; a re-caption can't cover them.
  • The picture goes black at 1:19 and the audio goes silent at 1:18.8. The last 23s are only leftover captions on black. All of it gets cut.
  • The audio is café-loud: mean −18 dB with peaks at −0.5 dB, plus kitchen clatter and a server walking through frame at about 0:45. Plan for light denoise and a dialogue EQ.
  • The background is green neon tile, which fights the brand's “no neon” rule. Pull green saturation down about 30% in the grade so the clay graphics carry the colour.
  • The niche is “US stocks / trading”. Whisper misheard “US stocks” as “I was stopped”, but the old captions confirm it. Captions will read “Trading. US stocks.”
Compliance call. At 1:10 you say “you make back ROI already”, but at 0:52 she says “almost”. I cut your ROI line and keep her “Almost, yeah.” It's the more believable beat, and it keeps Meta's earnings-claim review off our back. The end card also carries “Individual result. Results vary.”

Sound design

LayerSpecWhy
DialogueLight RNNoise denoise, high-pass at 90 Hz, +2 dB at 3 kHz for presence, then a leveller. Integrated −14 LUFS, true peak −1 dBTP.Café clatter tires viewers out. −14 LUFS is the Meta and YouTube playback target.
Music bedWarm acoustic/lo-fi at 88–96 BPM with no vocals. Sits about −26 LUFS under speech with sidechain ducking (−8 dB). Starts at the hook's 3.0s, swells into the 1:05 congrats, and resolves on the end card.Founders read slow and warm as credible. A trap or EDM bed reads like a get-rich ad.
SFX stylePaper, pencil and soft-UI sounds. No airhorns, bass drops or cash-register clichés. Every SFX sits 6–10 dB under dialogue.It matches the brand's paper-and-ink world and keeps the ad believable.
Room toneLoop 2s of clean café tone under every jump cut.Hides the cuts so they don't “pop”.
